\headline{Turn Budget Expanded to Fifteen Minutes}

\subhead{Long-running document turns now get ten minutes of work plus a five-minute headroom before the pause button appears.}

\byline{By Company Brain Staff}

The turn wall-clock deadline has moved from ten minutes to fifteen minutes, with the cooperative soft pause arming at ten minutes instead of seven. This gives slow PDF builds, web research, and sandbox compiles a real chance to finish inside a single chunk instead of pausing at the step boundary.

\columnbreak

\headline{LaTeX Newspaper and Comic Templates}

\subhead{Two new deterministic LaTeX templates compile inside the company-brain sandbox using only pre-installed packages.}

\byline{By Document Pipeline Team}

The sandbox ships texlive-latex-base, texlive-latex-recommended, texlive-fonts-recommended, and latexmk. The new newspaper and comic templates avoid texlive-latex-extra packages such as titlesec, enumitem, and lastpage so every build succeeds without missing .sty files.

\columnbreak

\headline{Flow MCP Images Stay Out of PDF Covers}

\subhead{Report covers are rendered in-sandbox with TikZ, never sent to external image models.}

\byline{By Safety Team}

PDF covers must be deterministic and sandboxed. Flow MCP runs Google Flow in a real Chrome browser on the operator's machine and cannot run inside the Worker sandbox. Cover art for newspaper and comic documents is drawn with TikZ or reused from the workspace hero, then embedded as a verified PNG before the compile.
